# Paper types and venue emphasis

Use paper type to choose the argument and venue to adapt scope and readership. These profiles combine labeled editorial scope summaries with this skill's writing heuristics; they are not fixed house-style recipes or guarantees of fit.

## Paper-type choices

| Type | Foreground | Evidence to preserve |
|---|---|---|
| Theory and information limits | Object, assumptions, theorem/bound, relation to known results, proof idea | Conditions, quantifiers, achievability/converse distinctions, tightness or remaining gap |
| Performance analysis | Metric, model, characterization, interpretation, design consequence | Derivation, approximations, limiting cases, optional numerical verification |
| Optimization and algorithms | Decision, obstacle, design principle, operations, information budget | Actual guarantee, feasibility, complexity, suitable comparisons |
| Learning | Communication decision, role of learning, observations, deployment conditions | Data split, information fairness, training/inference costs, generalization scope |
| Experimental systems | Operational question, system/measurement design, observed behavior | Calibration, repeatability, comparison setup, scale and environmental limits |
| Surveys | Scope, selection basis, taxonomy, critical synthesis | Traceable literature and balanced comparison; no invented systematic coverage |

These types overlap. Do not assume that TWC means simulation, TIT means proofs only, or ICC means a shortened journal paper.

## TWC

**Official scope summary:** IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications covers theoretical and practical wireless advances. Its policy requires the wireless communication problem and consequential channel characteristics to be central, rather than an incidental application label.

**Writing heuristic:** explain which channel, interference, information or resource constraint produces the question. Show how the analysis or design addresses it. A generic optimizer with a wireless label needs an actual communication-specific argument. State the model and scope of the claimed benefit.

**Official organization guidance, checked 2026-10-11:** the Introduction motivates and positions the contribution and summarizes its outcomes; the conclusion emphasizes takeaways beyond repeating the abstract.

Source: [TWC policies and guidelines](https://www.comsoc.org/publications/journals/ieee-twc/policies-guidelines).

## TCom / TCOM

**Official scope summary:** IEEE Transactions on Communications includes wireless, wired, optical and quantum communications. Classical communication topics emphasize the physical layer; cross-layer work requires an appropriate physical-layer basis. Its guidance emphasizes clear expression and logical progression.

**Writing heuristic:** define the communication problem, distinguish the contribution from a generic mathematical tool, and align assumptions, metric and comparisons. Do not replace all systems with a wireless template; coding, optical links and other settings require their own models.

Sources: [TCom scope](https://www.comsoc.org/publications/journals/ieee-transactions-communications) and [policies](https://www.comsoc.org/publications/journals/ieee-tcom/policies-and-guidelines), checked 2026-10-11.

## TIT

**Scope orientation:** IEEE Transactions on Information Theory concerns information transmission, processing and use, with conceptual and analytical contributions across information theory and related fields.

**Writing heuristic:** foreground the mathematical question, what is established, conditions, relation to known results, and proof strategy. A bound, converse, characterization, counterexample or conceptual distinction can be the main contribution. Do not demand a new algorithm, a throughput plot, or an engineering deployment claim when the paper's contribution is theoretical.

Canonical source for current scope and submission details: [TIT journal page](https://www.itsoc.org/it-trans). This reference does not certify current submission rules.

## ICC and GLOBECOM

**Venue orientation:** technical material is organized into edition-specific symposia and workshops. Choose the actual year, symposium/workshop and submission stage before checking compliance.

**Writing heuristic:** make a focused contribution independently understandable. Preserve the key model, result, method/proof and evidence; compress duplicated motivation and secondary detail first. Do not use a universal paragraph count, contribution count or experiment menu.

Official entry examples: [ICC 2026 author information](https://icc2026.ieee-icc.org/authors) and [GLOBECOM 2026 submission guidelines](https://globecom2026.ieee-globecom.org/submission-guidelines). These are edition-specific starting points, not permanent requirements. Full conference-rule pages could not all be retrieved during package preparation; no exact constraints are asserted here.

## Current-policy checks

For submission-readiness tasks, retrieve current official instructions for the exact target. Confirm article type, stage, length/format, anonymity, supplementary material, extension/disclosure and other requested constraints. If a page is inaccessible, mark the specific check unverified and continue the writing work.

Do not hard-code page limits, abstract limits, deadlines, fees or anonymization rules as timeless facts. Do not infer an IEEE-wide rule from one journal or conference edition. Distinguish author/user preferences from actual editorial requirements.

For conference-to-journal extensions, assess and describe the actual added technical contribution. The TWC and TCom policy pages checked on 2026-10-11 do not impose a universal numerical percentage threshold. Do not invent a “30% new material” requirement; recheck the current target's policy for an actual submission.
